## LinkedIn post — option A (recommended)

A couple of us once decided to build our first real software together: an app for chatting with SEC filings. You'd point it at a company's annual report, ask a question, and get an answer pulled straight from the document rather than from whatever the model vaguely remembered.

We built the whole thing — a retrieval pipeline over EDGAR, an agent that chose its own tools, accounts, payments, deployment, the lot. We got it live, learned an enormous amount, and eventually let it rest.

I finally wrote up the honest version: what it did, the one design decision that let two of us build in parallel without tripping over each other, and the mistakes — starting with the API keys we committed straight into the repo.

It's not a success story exactly. But it was the first thing I built *with* other people, and that turned out to be the real lesson.
